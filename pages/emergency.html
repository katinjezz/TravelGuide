What To Do In An Emergency

🚑

Medical Emergency

Call 911 immediately. Contact your travel insurance provider. Visit the nearest emergency room.

👮

Lost Passport

Report it to the police. Contact the Kenyan Embassy. Carry your passport copies.

💳

Lost Bank Card

Freeze the card immediately. Contact your bank. Use your backup card.

🚕

Transport Problems

Use Uber. Contact the hotel concierge. Avoid accepting unofficial taxis.

Safety Tips

📱 Keep Phone Charged

Carry a power bank every day.

💵 Carry Limited Cash

Use cards whenever possible.

📍 Share Your Location

Let family members know where you are.

👜 Watch Your Belongings

Keep valuables close in crowded places.

🏨 Return Before Late

Use Uber if travelling after dark.

❤️ Stay Calm

Most situations can be resolved by contacting hotel staff or emergency services.

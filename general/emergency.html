Keep the embassy address, phone number and website saved on your phone before travelling.

🛂 Lost Passport

Report the loss to the local police immediately, contact the Kenyan Embassy or Consulate, and follow their instructions to obtain an emergency travel document or replacement passport.

📄 Important Copies

Store digital copies of passports, visas, travel insurance and flight confirmations securely online and offline.

Travel Safety Tips

Simple habits that help keep everyone safe.

Protect Valuables

Keep passports and valuables in the hotel safe whenever possible.

Share Your Location

Let family members know where you're going, especially when exploring separately.

Keep Phones Charged

Carry a fully charged power bank during sightseeing.

Know Your Hotel

Save the hotel address in your phone and keep a printed copy just in case.

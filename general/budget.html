Money Saving Tips

Spend wisely throughout your journey.

💳 Use Your Card

Use a travel-friendly debit or credit card where possible instead of carrying large amounts of cash.

🛒 Shop Smart

Compare prices before buying souvenirs and take advantage of outlet malls.

🍽 Plan Meals

Mix restaurant dining with supermarkets and casual eateries to manage food costs.

Before Departure Checklist

Complete these financial tasks before travelling.

□ Notify your bank of international travel.

□ Confirm your travel insurance.

□ Carry some US Dollars for emergencies.

□ Keep a backup payment method.
